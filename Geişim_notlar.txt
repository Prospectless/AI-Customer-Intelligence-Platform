#########################################################

# TOTALSPENT FEATURE ENGINEERING

#########################################################

1. ProductCategory bazlı ortalama sepet tutarı tanımlandı:

* Electronics → 150
* Furniture → 200
* Fashion → 60
* Groceries → 30
* Sports → 80
* Kitchen → 50

2. CustomerSegment bazlı segment çarpanı oluşturuldu:

* Regular → 1.0
* Premium → 1.3
* VIP → 1.7

3. Discount etkisi hesaplandı:

discount_factor =
1 - (DiscountsAvailed × 0.05)

* Minimum değer: 0.6
* Maksimum değer: 1.0

4. Loyalty etkisi hesaplandı:

* LoyaltyProgram = 1 → 1.1 bonus
* LoyaltyProgram = 0 → 1.0

5. TotalSpent değişkeni hesaplandı:

TotalSpent =
avg_basket
× segment_multiplier
× discount_factor
× loyalty_bonus
× NumberOfPurchases

6. Ara kolonlar silindi:

* avg_basket
* seg_mult
* discount_factor
* loyalty_bonus

7. Yeni dataset oluşturuldu:

* customerData_500k_v2.csv

Bu aşamadan sonra tüm analizler
customerData_500k_v2.csv
üzerinden gerçekleştirildi.
